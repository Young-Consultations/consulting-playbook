# Active Software Delivery Assessment pilot

This is the canonical active commercial-validation offer for portfolio task
[#166](https://github.com/Young-Consultations/portfolio-tasks/issues/166),
superseding the executable intent of consulting issue
[#86](https://github.com/Young-Consultations/consulting-playbook/issues/86).
All commercial values, buyer/channel choices, delivery effort, and outreach
inputs are unvalidated hypotheses. Repository assets prove availability of
methods, not market demand, benchmark pricing, willingness-to-pay, or repeatability.

## Pilot definition

| Item | Selected experiment input |
| --- | --- |
| Offer | Software Delivery Assessment pilot |
| Price hypothesis | $750 fixed fee; test willingness-to-pay rather than assert it. |
| Payment hypothesis | $375 booking / $375 delivery. |
| Scope | Exactly one delayed feature, integration handoff, or release decision; identify its boundary and desired decision before booking. |
| Turnaround | Five business days after agreed sanitized inputs are received and scope is confirmed; record the start and delivery dates. Missing inputs require an agreed schedule reset. |
| Kickoff | Proposed 30 minutes to confirm the decision, boundary, evidence, and owners. |
| Evidence limit | No more than five suitable sanitized artifacts, agreed before booking. |
| Findings limit | No more than three evidence-backed findings; disclose gaps rather than infer unsupported causes. |
| Outputs | Concise delivery map and prioritized two-week action plan with owners and verification steps. |
| Readout | Proposed 45 minutes to discuss findings, actions, uncertainty, and the buyer's decision. |
| Revision | One clarification revision of the delivered report, within the agreed scope; no new investigation or evidence collection. Agree its request and return dates in the SOW. |
| Delivery effort | Five-hour consultant delivery-effort cap, including preparation, kickoff, evidence review, analysis, report, readout, and clarification revision. |
| Buyer hypothesis | Engineering or delivery leader in a small software organization with authority to buy advisory help and act on a concrete delivery decision within 30 days. Validate role, urgency, authority, and budget. |
| Secondary consultant channel | Independent consultants needing a bounded assessment for their client; confirm end-client authority, evidence permissions, and responsibilities. Channel demand is unvalidated. |
| AI positioning | Contextual only when relevant to the problem and permitted evidence; AI is not a mandatory sales theme or assessment domain. |

Kickoff/readout durations are provisional operational choices because task #166
specifies no numeric durations. A proposed five-hour budget is: preparation and
kickoff 0.75 h, review/map 1.25 h, findings/report/actions 1.75 h, readout 0.75 h,
and revision 0.5 h. Test actual effort; do not silently exceed the cap. If fit
fails, stop and agree reduced scope, defer, or separately propose a larger engagement.

## Explicit exclusions

No implementation, production code changes, system access, exhaustive code or
security audit, security/compliance certification, legal advice, personnel
performance evaluation, multi-team maturity scoring, broad AI transformation,
30/60/90 roadmap, implementation backlog, or ongoing delivery management.
No additional problems, artifacts, findings, interviews, or revisions within
this pilot. Larger work requires separate scope and authorization.

Use only generic or appropriately authorized sanitized evidence. Do not place
client-specific, employer-sensitive, private analytics, credentials,
export-controlled, or private evidence in this repository or executable prompts.
Do not require raw repository access or sensitive artifacts to qualify.
