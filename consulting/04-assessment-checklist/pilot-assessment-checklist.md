# Pilot assessment checklist

Apply the limits in the [canonical pilot](../01-service-offer/active-software-delivery-assessment-pilot.md).
Use the [qualification gates](../02-client-intake/client-qualification-checklist.md)
before booking. Keep engagement records in an authorized workspace; this is a generic template.

- [ ] Confirm one decision/problem, desired action within 30 days, sponsor, and boundary at kickoff.
- [ ] Agree no more than five sanitized artifacts, limited to the evidence necessary for the selected decision; do not request raw sensitive evidence by default.
- [ ] For each evidence item, record in an authorized evidence register: evidence/artifact ID; source/provenance and collection date; custodian where appropriate; classification; permitted use/purpose; transfer-review status; permitted destination(s); named retention/disposal responsibility; and limitations/validation status. Record handling decisions and unresolved restrictions, including authorized access, required redaction, attribution, and retention period/disposal timing. Mark conditional fields not applicable with a reason rather than leaving them blank.
- [ ] Treat unknown classification as restricted pending review. Do not transfer it until classification and handling have been reviewed and the transfer authorized for the intended purpose and destination; record the reviewer, decision, and date in the transfer-review status.
- [ ] Map the delivery path from request to the selected decision, with handoffs, owners, blockers, and unknowns; avoid organization-wide maturity scoring.
- [ ] Separate observations from interpretations; corroborate each finding with artifact references and state confidence and alternative explanations.
- [ ] Select no more than three findings by decision impact and urgency; mark unsupported questions as limitations.
- [ ] Create prioritized two-week actions with accountable owners, timing, dependencies, and observable verification steps.
- [ ] Produce the [pilot report](../07-report-template/pilot-assessment-report.md), exposing classification, permitted use, and transfer-review status on the report and any other knowledge or handoff artifact that may contain engagement information; verify handling restrictions before readout and capture the buyer's decision.
- [ ] Reserve effort for one clarification revision; track total delivery hours against the five-hour cap. Escalate fit failures before expanding work.
- [ ] Record sanitized aggregate learning in the [scoreboard](../01-service-offer/commercial-validation-scoreboard.md), including non-sales and effort overruns.

AI-related analysis is optional and contextual. Do not add an AI domain unless it
helps explain this decision with suitable evidence.
