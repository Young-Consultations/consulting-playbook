# Software Delivery Assessment pilot report

Follow the [canonical pilot](../01-service-offer/active-software-delivery-assessment-pilot.md)
and [assessment checklist](../04-assessment-checklist/pilot-assessment-checklist.md).
Complete this in an authorized engagement workspace, not in the public repository.
Keep private/client information and evidence registers in that workspace; do not
store them in this repository or request raw sensitive evidence by default.

## Report handling

Record this report's classification, permitted use/purpose, transfer-review
status (reviewer, decision, and date), permitted destination(s), authorized
access, and named retention/disposal responsibility and timing. Record required
redaction, attribution, handling decisions, and unresolved restrictions. Expose
classification, permitted use, and transfer-review status on every derived
knowledge or handoff artifact that may contain engagement information.

Unknown classification is restricted pending review. Do not transfer an artifact
until its classification and handling have been reviewed and transfer authorized
for the intended purpose and destination.

## Decision and evidence boundary

Record the single delayed feature, integration handoff, or release decision;
buyer/decision owner; intended action within 30 days; agreed input and delivery
dates; scope; and limitations. Inventory at most five sanitized artifacts,
limited to evidence necessary for this decision. For each evidence item, record
the following fields here or explicitly reference its entry in an authorized
evidence register containing all these fields:

- Evidence/artifact ID; source/provenance and collection date; custodian where appropriate.
- Classification; permitted use/purpose; transfer-review status (reviewer, decision, and date); permitted destination(s).
- Named retention/disposal responsibility; limitations/validation status.
- Handling decisions and unresolved restrictions, including authorized access, required redaction, attribution, and retention period/disposal timing.

Mark conditional fields not applicable with a reason rather than leaving them
blank. Record unknowns explicitly and apply the restricted handling rule above.
Ensure report recipients are authorized to access any referenced register;
otherwise carry the required handling fields in the inventory itself.

## Evidence-backed findings (maximum three)

For each finding record: priority; observation; supporting artifact IDs and
locators; interpretation and confidence; alternative explanation or gap;
customer/business impact; and implication for the selected decision. Do not
force three findings when evidence supports fewer.

## Readout and clarification

Record buyer decision, accepted limitations, action ownership, unresolved questions,
and the one clarification revision requested/completed on agreed dates. New
investigation or implementation needs separate scope. Record consultant delivery
hours, including the reserved revision effort, against the five-hour cap.
